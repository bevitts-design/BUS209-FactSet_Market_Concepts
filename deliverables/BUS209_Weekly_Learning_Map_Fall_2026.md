## Items to settle before issuing a definitive student checklist

1. **Certification timing:** Canvas says Dec. 11 at 11:30 p.m.; the syllabus says finals week Dec. 14–18. This map uses the earlier Canvas deadline. The three assignment titles are known, but their empty descriptions do not specify acceptable completion evidence or the exact choice for “Additional or Office Integration.”
2. **Fixed income:** Nov. 9 and Nov. 16 remain TBD in the live syllabus. The Nov. 30 presentation is scheduled, but its Canvas module is empty and there is no corresponding assignment. Define FI/FX scope and submission expectations.
3. **Final deliverable:** The Dec. 7 Canvas assignment exists but has an empty description and text-entry submission. The syllabus calls for a paper and presentation. The Final Exam/ Project module is empty even though the assignment exists in Assignments.
4. **Scavenger hunt:** The introductory deck offers a generic four-point company snapshot; Canvas assigns a detailed Walmart hunt. Teach the snapshot as a warm-up and use the Canvas task for submission.
5. **Country presentation duration:** The assignment prompt says 5–8 minutes; its rubric says 5–10. A 5–8-minute target fits both, but the wording should eventually be aligned.
6. **Instructional coverage:** Add targeted instruction for indicator quality, index-return calculations, the four yield-curve shifts, currency-risk management, and the scheduled ESG component. The Canvas Stock Pitch and Industry Analysis attachments have not been inspected in full, so they may contain additional coverage.

These are review findings; no Canvas settings, assignments, dates, or teaching decks were changed.
