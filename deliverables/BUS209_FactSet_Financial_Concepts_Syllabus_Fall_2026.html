Catalog Description

FactSet Financial Concepts (FFC) introduces the characteristics of four core areas of finance. The core areas covered are Economic Indicators, Currencies, Fixed Income, and Equities. The sections are woven together from FactSet data, news, and analytics. Students, individually or in small groups, will make class presentations using the FactSet terminal on at least one topic covered in each section.

What You’ll Learn

  1. Explain how investors use economic indicators to gauge the health of the economy.
  2. Explain the qualities of good economic indicators.
  3. Identify the three main drivers of currency valuation.
  4. Demonstrate how investors and businesses are affected by currency markets and how they manage currency risk.
  5. Describe how yields facilitate comparison across the vast diversity of the bond market.
  6. Interpret the meaning of the four major shifts in the yield curve and the meaning of curve inversion.
  7. Calculate equity index performance from the performance of single stocks.
  8. Identify the three types of relative valuation and the role of future earnings growth when assessing fair value.

How We’ll Learn

Course sessions include lecture, group discussions, and hands-on FactSet terminal exercises, both in class and for homework.

Stay connected: Check email and Canvas at least once per day. Keep up with assignments, ask questions when concepts are unclear, and use the instructor’s support.

Readings & Materials

  • FactSet modules in video form.
  • FactSet Certificate programs are free for students who access learning information through the FactSet app/web pages.
  • Required: FactSet Desktop Application and Microsoft Office 365.

Evaluation & Grading

Student learning will be assessed through core section deliverables and presentations (80%), FactSet Certifications (10%), and logistics, timeliness, preparedness, attendance, and participation (10%). For the four core sections, each student or group—depending on class size—will demonstrate proficiency using the FactSet terminal to access and explain relevant financial information. Deliverables include PowerPoint submissions and verbal class presentations using the FactSet terminal.
