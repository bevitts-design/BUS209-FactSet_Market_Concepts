BUS209 · Economic outlook
13

Snapshot versus direction

Separate the latest reading from the emerging trend

Historical FactSet outlook view from the 2025 source deck.

Ask three questions

Level: Is the latest value strong or weak?

Direction: Is the series improving, deteriorating, or flat?

Consistency: Do related indicators tell the same story?

Do not stop at the last column: your conclusion should explain the pattern across periods.
