BUS209 · Valuation framework
05

Two routes to a defensible range

Absolute valuation builds value from cash flows; relative valuation infers it from comparisons

Absolute

Discounted cash flow

Forecast the cash the business can generate and discount it for time and risk.

Relative

Comparable valuation

Compare the company’s price or enterprise value with a relevant financial metric and peer set.

Best practice: use more than one method and investigate why the answers differ.

BUS209 · Present value
07

You try it

Time and risk reduce the value of a distant cash flow

Present value = cash flow ÷ (1 + WACC)t

The exponent t is the number of periods until the cash flow arrives.

Cash flow: $100 million

WACC: 10%

Timing: Year 5

$100m ÷ (1.10)5
≈ $62.1 million today
BUS209 · Discount rate
08

Weighted average cost of capital

WACC blends the required returns of equity and debt investors

WACC = (equity weight × cost of equity) + (debt weight × after-tax cost of debt)
Equity capital

Higher uncertainty requires a higher return

CAPM connects market risk, company beta, and the risk-free rate.

Debt capital

Lenders price default and interest-rate risk

Use the company’s borrowing cost after the tax benefit of interest.

BUS209 · Cost of equity
09

Calculate with CAPM

Beta scales the market risk premium to the stock’s sensitivity

Cost of equity = risk-free rate + beta × market risk premium

Risk-free rate: 4.2%

Beta: 1.20

Market risk premium: 5.9%

4.2% + (1.20 × 5.9%)
≈ 11.3% cost of equity

Illustrative inputs for classroom calculation; use current sourced inputs in an actual valuation.

BUS209 · Relative valuation
12

Three related valuation lenses

Choose a metric that matches the economic question

Income return

Dividend yield

Dividend per share ÷ Price

Useful when distributions are a meaningful part of the investment case.

Earnings return

Earnings yield

EPS ÷ Price

The earnings generated for each dollar invested in the stock.

Price paid

P/E ratio

Price ÷ EPS

The reciprocal of earnings yield when the same earnings measure is used.
