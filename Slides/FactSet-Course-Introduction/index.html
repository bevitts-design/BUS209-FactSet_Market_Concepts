Why FactSet matters

1Markets move faster than an analyst can rebuild every dataset from scratch.
2Professionals need data, context, and meaningful comparisons—not isolated numbers.
3The advantage comes from turning evidence into a clear decision or recommendation.

BUS209 · Course content
06

What you will learn

Four finance lenses shape the questions we ask

EI

Economic indicators

Use leading, coincident, and lagging information to gauge the health and direction of an economy.

FX

Currencies

Explain the forces behind currency values and how investors and businesses manage currency risk.

FI

Fixed income

Compare yields across bonds and interpret what movements in the yield curve may signal.

EQ

Equities

Connect company and index performance to relative valuation, growth expectations, and fair value.

BUS209 · Learning model
07

How we will learn

Every class moves from concepts to evidence to explanation

Learn

Build the financial concept and complete FactSet certification modules.

Practice

Use FactSet in class to find, compare, and interpret real financial information.

Present

Explain what the evidence means through live demonstrations and professional deliverables.

The goal is not to memorize tabs. The goal is to use the platform to answer a financial question clearly.
