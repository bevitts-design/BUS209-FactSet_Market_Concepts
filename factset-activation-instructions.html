Activate FactSet and Add It to Excel

Complete both parts below before you need FactSet for a class assignment.

Before you begin: Use your Endicott email address and have your anticipated graduation year ready.

PART 1

Activate your FactSet account

  1. Open the FactSet Academic Procurement page.
    Open FactSet Activation Page ↗
  2. Choose Student and sign up with your Endicott email address.
  3. Enter your anticipated graduation year when prompted, then submit your request.
  4. After your request is approved, follow the FactSet email instructions to finish setting up your user ID and password.
How you will use FactSet: Endicott students use FactSet for the Web. You may work in a browser or download the FactSet web application.
PART 2

Add FactSet 365 to Excel

Complete these steps after you can sign in to FactSet. Google Chrome is recommended for installing and using the FactSet 365 add-in.

  1. Go to Office.com ↗ and sign in with your Endicott account. You may also be asked to sign in to OneDrive.
  2. Open a new or existing Excel workbook.
  3. In the Excel ribbon, follow this path:
    Home  →  Add-ins  →  More Add-ins
  4. In the add-ins window, follow this path:
    Store  →  Search “FactSet 365”  →  Add
  5. Sign in to FactSet from within Excel. The add-in functions will not work until you are signed in.
